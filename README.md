# abs-strategic-account-dashboard
Interactive ROI and herd performance dashboard demonstrating technical service value, genetic improvement, and heat stress mitigation for strategic dairy accounts.
# ABS Global — Technical Services Account Dashboard

**Live Demo:** [Click here to view the interactive dashboard](https://grvdutta96.github.io/abs-strategic-account-dashboard/)

## Overview
This interactive dashboard is a prototype developed to demonstrate the technical consultation, data analysis, and commercial validation capabilities required for the **Technical Services Sr. Specialist (East Region)** role at ABS Global / Genus.

Designed from the perspective of a technical specialist reviewing a strategic account (a 1,850-cow Holstein dairy in Pennsylvania), this tool bridges the gap between complex genetic/phenotypic data and actionable, on-farm profitability.

## Motivation
As a DVM and quantitative geneticist, I built this dashboard to showcase my ability to execute the core responsibilities of the Technical Services program:
1. **Identification of technical opportunities** that optimize customer profitability.
2. **Monitoring of progress and impact** of technical solutions (before vs. after implementation).
3. **Preparation of performance summaries** and product validation data for strategic customers.

## Key Features & Views

*   **Reproductive Performance Benchmarking:** Tracks trailing 12-month 21-day pregnancy rates and conception rates against ABS benchmarks. Includes pen-level performance breakdowns to isolate bottlenecks (e.g., fresh cow vs. repeat breeders) and corresponding clinical SOPs.
*   **Heat Stress × Conception Mitigation:** Overlays barn THI with conception rate drops. Leverages my Ph.D. research on GxE heat stress models to quantify exact milk/reproductive losses and recommend actionable heat abatement and seasonal synchronization strategies.
*   **Genetic Improvement Trends:** Visualizes 5-year rolling daughter performance for TPI, Net Merit $, and DPR. Includes a sire selection audit to demonstrate how switching to ABS high-fertility and heat-tolerant sires directly improves herd metrics.
*   **Dynamic ROI Calculator:** An interactive tool allowing users to adjust herd size, milk price, and days open to calculate the tangible financial impact (Total Annual Impact) of ABS technical services. 

## Technical Details
This is a lightweight, single-page application (SPA) built for cross-device compatibility, ensuring it can be viewed on a desktop during a corporate review or on a tablet/mobile device while walking a freestall barn.
*   **Technologies:** HTML5, CSS3 (Vanilla), JavaScript
*   **Data Visualization:** Chart.js (Line, Bar, and Dual-Axis charts)
